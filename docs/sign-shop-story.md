# Sign-shop presentation context

## Purpose

Krush's account gives the presentation a concrete personal connection. It does not change the project scope, roadmap, evaluation, or acceptance gates. The challenge and general structured-design reflow goals drive development. Any benefit to the family business is a possible outcome, not a project obligation.

## Reported context

Krush described two starting points: a designer creates artwork from a client brief, or prepares artwork supplied by a client. Preparation can involve vectorization, quality inspection, and sizing. Clients may request multiple design iterations before approval, followed by the shop's production and installation work.

The most relevant detail for our story is that the same company artwork may need adaptation for a large sign, yard sign, banner, and flag with different proportions. This is a reported workflow, not an observed study or evidence that the business uses Reflow.

## How to use it in the presentation

Suggested opening:

> Krush's family runs a sign shop. He described how the same artwork must be adapted for a large sign, yard sign, banner, and flag. That gives a concrete example of the broader problem behind Reflow Studio: adapting a prepared design to different proportions while preserving its content, identity, and editability.

Then demonstrate the general system on a supported structured design. Use varied rectangular canvas proportions and show the original, a baseline, and the selected layouts. A composition with text, artwork, and meaningful groups makes the reflow behavior easier to judge than scaling a lone logo. Any example artwork or dimensions we invent should be identified as illustrative.

The story should point to capabilities we can actually demonstrate. It does not imply support for every sign shape or the business's complete workflow. The account supplies no measured savings, adoption, or accepted Reflow output, so the presentation should claim none.

## Scope remains unchanged

- Build and evaluate a general automatic reflow engine on varied, independent designs.
- Address preservation, typography, visibility, layout quality, stability, runtime, and native editability through the existing technical backlog.
- Develop the optional campaign-correction workflow only under its existing evidence and readiness gates.
- Keep artwork reconstruction, image enhancement, sign mockups, and printing/installation preparation outside this project.

No shop interview, pilot, customer dataset, business-specific feature, or follow-up task is created by this account. No additional information from the family is needed to continue development.
